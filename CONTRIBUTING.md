# Contributing to Awesome Agentic Advertising

Thank you for your interest in contributing to **Awesome Agentic Advertising**! We welcome community contributions that expand and enrich the ecosystem of AI-powered advertising tools, open protocols, and developer resources.

## Curation Principles

To maintain the highest quality, authority, and developer utility, all submissions must align with the following criteria:

### In Scope
- **Official Ad Platform APIs & Official MCP Servers**: Direct developer APIs, official SDKs, and official MCP implementations provided by major ad platforms (Google, Meta, Amazon, Microsoft, TikTok, etc.).
- **Open Protocols & Industry Standards**: Open standards governed by industry bodies or open-source consortia (Model Context Protocol, IAB OpenRTB, IAB VAST, C2PA).
- **Ad Tech & Protocol Validation**: Open-source format validators, syntax linters, and verification tooling for programmatic advertising specs (e.g., `vastlint`, `rtblint`).
- **Foundational Generative AI Models**: Core image, video, audio, and speech generation models suitable for programmatic creative production (e.g., Imagen, Veo, FLUX, ElevenLabs).
- **Server-Side Conversion APIs**: Official server-to-server conversion interfaces (CAPI, Enhanced Conversions, Events API).
- **Scientific Attribution & Simulation**: Open-source Media Mix Modeling (MMM), incrementality, synthetic controls, and auction simulation frameworks (e.g., Meridian, Robyn, CausalML).
- **Agent Frameworks & Developer Tooling**: Open-source agent orchestration runtimes, developer environments, and multi-agent coordination frameworks (e.g., LangGraph, CrewAI, AutoGen).

### Out of Scope
- **Commercial Competitors & Proprietary Agency Software**: Commercial multi-platform ad management SaaS platforms, proprietary third-party agencies, and commercial automation wrappers will not be accepted.
- **Closed or Unreleased Tools**: Tools that are not publicly available or require proprietary beta access without public documentation.
- **Affiliate Links & Commercial Spam**: Entries containing affiliate codes, paywalled self-promotion, or undisclosed commercial tracking.

---

## Submission Checklist

Before opening a pull request, please verify:

- [ ] **Category Fit**: The submission fits one of the existing sections or clearly justifies a new one.
- [ ] **Formatting**: The entry follows standard markdown format:
  ```markdown
  - **[Project Name](https://example.com)** - Concise 1-2 sentence description highlighting technical capability.
  ```
- [ ] **Link Reachability**: The target website or repository is active, resolves with HTTP 200, and has public documentation.
- [ ] **Neutral Tone**: Descriptions should be objective, technical, and free of superlative marketing copy ("best", "revolutionary").
